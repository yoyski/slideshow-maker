FAQ

Do I need a screen recorder?

No. You add slides as pictures and record your voice on them. The app makes the video.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Slides and recordings are processed in your browser and stay on your device. We store only your name, email and a scrambled copy of your password, so you can sign in.

What can I use as slides?

Any pictures: PNG, JPG, WebP and similar. From PowerPoint, choose File, Export, then PNG for all slides.

How long does downloading take?

The video is recorded in real time, so it takes about as long as the video itself. Keep the tab open until it finishes.

Which file do I get?

An MP4 when your browser can make one. Otherwise a WebM, which plays in most players and browsers.
